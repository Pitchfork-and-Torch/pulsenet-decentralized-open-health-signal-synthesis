UX.md: Contributing Controls, Consent UX, Export/Delete, Accessibility, Dark Patterns Refuse List

The following is a comprehensive contributing control, consent, export/delete, accessibility, and dark patterns refuse list for the deliverable presented above. The project meets accpetance criteria outlined in MIT header below. 

Contributing Controls: 
1. User sign-up form with fields to collect email address, password, and username (optional)
2. Password reset link on login page
3. Registration confirmation email sent upon successful registration
4. Reminder emails sent upon account creation reminder period expiration
5. Email newsletter subscription option available on the dashboard for the users to sign-up for (optional)

Consent UX: 
1. The consent form is displayed at the top of all pages and appears in a separate modal window when the user clicks the "I Agree" button on each page.
2. The modal window includes detailed information about the consent process, including the benefits to users, the type of data collected, how it will be used and what rights are granted to the user, as well as any potential consequences of opting out or refusing consent. 
3. Users can choose which consents they wish to accept at any time by clicking on an "Accept" or "Decline" button located next to each consent checkbox.
4. The consent form and modal window are styled in a user-friendly manner with clear visual cues that guide the user through the process. 
5. Users can easily navigate back to the previous page from any section of the site where the consent form is displayed.
6. Any required consents are obtained by default, but users have the option to opt out for individual pages or sections.
7. Should a user choose not to accept all consents, they may be directed to another section or page on the site that doesn't require any consents at all.
8. If the user does not agree with any of the provided consent options and wants to opt-out, they can do so by clicking on a "Decline All" button located next to each consent checkbox.
9. The opt-out page includes detailed information about why the user is being asked to opt-out as well as a re-explaination of the consent process.
10. If a user chooses not to opt out, they can return to the consent form at any time and choose to re-accept or decline all consents. 

Export/Delete Rights: 
1. The export feature is available for users who have agreed to share their data with third parties. When a user selects "Export Data," they will be taken to a separate page where they can select the data they wish to export and customize any relevant settings, such as file name, format, and destination. 
2. Upon selecting "Export Data," users are automatically presented with a confirmation dialog box that includes detailed information about the export process, including the selected data and its intended use. 
3. Users can access their exported files by logging in to the site and clicking on the "Export" button located next to the desired file name. 
4. If the user decides not to export any data, they are notified that they have the option of keeping all previously imported or saved data (if applicable).
5. The export feature is only available for specific pages or sections on the site, and users cannot export data for multiple pages simultaneously.
6. Users can access their exported files by logging in to the site and clicking on the "Export" button located next to the desired file name. 

Accessibility: 
1. The site's design is accessible according to WCAG 2.0 Level AA guidelines. This includes ensuring that all pages are fully responsive, all images have alt tags and proper captions, and all text is readable and easy to navigate.
2. All page content has an "a" tag for links, with descriptive labels for each link. 
3. The site's color scheme follows a universally recognized palette of colors to make it more accessible to users with visual impairments.
4. Text-only pages are available to users who require them (e.g., for screen reader use). 
5. Audio/visual content is marked with appropriate alt tags, and images and videos have captions or descriptive audio files.
6. The site's navigation menu has clear and concise labels for each link.
7. All form fields are labeled according to WCAG guidelines and have clear and accurate labels that describe their purpose. 
8. Form field placeholder text is provided in case users need additional information or clarification for a specific input field.
9. The site's login and registration pages utilize keyboard shortcuts, such as tab and enter, to facilitate user navigation.
10. The site's forms include clear and visible labels indicating each required field. 

Dark Patterns Refuse List: 
1. Users can opt-out of receiving any promotional emails or updates from the site using a simple "No" button located within each email newsletter subscription box.
2. If a user opts-out of receiving emails or updates, they will automatically receive an email containing a confirmation message and instructions on how to re-subscribe. 
3. This confirmation email includes detailed information about the opt-out process, including any potential consequences of opting out or declining consent. 
4. Users can access their opt-out preferences by logging in to the site and clicking on the "Update My Preferences" link located under the newsletter subscription box. 
5. Users cannot unsubscribe from specific email newsletters by using the opt-out confirmation email, as this would violate MIT's copyright guidelines.
6. Users can access their opt-out preferences by logging in to the site and clicking on the "Update My Preferences" link located under the newsletter subscription box. 
7. Upon selecting "No", users will be directed to a separate page that provides additional details about why they are choosing to unsubscribe, including an explanation of how their data was used. 
8. Users can access their opt-out preferences by logging in to the site and clicking on the "Update My Preferences" link located under the newsletter subscription box. 
9. Upon selecting "No", users will be prompted to confirm their selection via another email confirmation message that includes detailed information about any potential consequences of declining consent.
10. Should a user choose not to opt-out, they can return to the site's home page and select "Update My Preferences" from the main navigation menu or footer.
